A plan reviewer runs only after the human approves the drafted design and plan; draft, present,
wait.

**Why:** 2026-11-16, ir-lifetimes: I spawned a cold plan reviewer right after drafting; the user
stopped it: "don't run reviewer until i approve".

**How to apply:** after writing a design/plan, present it to the human and stop; spawn the plan
reviewer (if any) only once they approve. Related: [[brief-review-first]], [[coordination]].
