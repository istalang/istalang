## Ranked Top 10 Bottlenecks

### 1. [NEW] Unmemoized Trait Conformance (`conform`) & Struct Constraint Checking (`structShortfall`) Causes $3^d$ Exponential Re-Checking on Nested Models
- **Phase & Workload:** Checker (`compile` / `ista check` and incremental `diagnosticsOf` on `examples/FormBuilder`).
- **Cost:** **69.99% of total `FormBuilder` check time** (`3,216 ms / 4,595 ms` over 3 runs) and **89.48% of `FormBuilder` incremental edit time**. Exponential $O(k^d)$ scaling in wrapper nesting depth $d$ ($k = 3$ required functions in `FormNode`). `fieldsOf` is called **9,011 times on only 251 unique struct types** (up to 1,104 times on one struct type); `parametersOf` is called **274,110 times**.
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/compiler/checker/resolution/conformance/conformance.ts`: `conform`, `functionWitnesses`, `witnessOf`, `witnessChosen`, `instantiateAt`, `witnessesOf`, `silentMeets`
  - `src/compiler/checker/resolution/conformance/shortfalls.ts`: `meetsStructConstraints`, `structShortfall`, `fieldsShortfall`, `ruleShortfall`
  - `src/compiler/checker/resolution/choosing/applicability.ts`: `takenBy` (28.2% total / 7.3% self), `argumentsInapplicability`, `judgedArgument`
  - `src/compiler/checker/resolution/choosing/binding.ts`: `argumentSlotsOf` (3.7% self)
- **Mechanism:** Neither `conform` nor `structShortfall` is memoized per `(typeKey, constraintKey)` / `(typeKey, structModelKey)`. In `FormBuilder`, `FormNode` has 3 required functions (`seen`, `read`, `load`), and `Group<S is struct { [_] is FormNode }>`, `List<R is FormNode>`, `Variant<C is struct { [_] is FormNode }>`, `Hidden<N is FormNode>` implement all 3 with the same constraint on their type parameter. Checking `Group<M> is FormNode` instantiates `seen<M>`, `read<M>`, and `load<M>`, each of which re-checks `M is struct { [_] is FormNode }` from scratch (3x per wrapper level = $3^d$ at depth $d$). Additionally, `ruleShortfall` (`shortfalls.ts:188`) eagerly evaluates `typeText(fieldType)` on every field check even when the field passes, and `checker.memberWitnesses` (`trait-objects.ts:45`) re-runs `witnessesFor` unmemoized during `lower`.
- **Proposed Root Fix (medium):** Memoize `enter` and `judge` outcomes in `Search` (`witness-outcomes.ts`) and memoize top-level conformance / `memberWitnesses` in `EntryTable`s on `ProgramChecker` keyed by `typeKey` and `constraintKey` / `structModelKey`; make `field` formatting lazy in `ruleShortfall`.

### 2. [NEW] `substitute` / `mapParts` / `rebuiltBy` / `associatedTypeOf` Allocate ~270,000 Duplicate `Type` Objects, Defeating `WeakMap` Caches (`ownKeys`, `recursionHeld`) and Re-Running `TermGraph` Minimization in `unionOf` / `folded`
- **Phase & Workload:** Lowering (`lower` on `examples/FormBuilder`) and Checker (`check` on `examples/FormBuilder`).
- **Cost:** **81.72% of total `lower` time** is spent inside `checker/types/` (`substitute`: **46.24%**, `typeKey`: **48.86%**, `unionOf`: **38.00%**, `folded`: **19.40%**, `recursive-terms.ts:#isTerm`: **8.42% self**, `structures/memo.ts:memo`: **19.65% self in `lower` / 22.96% self in `check`**). Across `diagnose` + `lower` of `FormBuilder`, there are only **2,716 unique types** (`uniqueTypeKeys`), yet **269,336 distinct `Type` objects** (~100 copies per type) are allocated and `typeKey` is called **1,423,708 times**.
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/substitution.ts`: `substitute`, `asWritten`, `substituteAny`
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/parts/type-parts.ts`: `mapChildren`, `rebuiltBy`, `mapParts`, `parametersIn`
  - `src/compiler/checker/traits/known-constraints.ts`: `associatedTypeOf`
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/unions/unions.ts`: `unionOf`
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/identity/recursive-types.ts`: `folded`
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/identity/recursive-terms.ts`: `#isTerm`, `slotOf`, `#spelled`, `canonicalKey`
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/identity/term-signatures.ts`: `rankedBy`
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/instantiation.ts`: `instantiatedBody`
- **Mechanism:**
  1. `mapParts`, `rebuiltBy`, and `asWritten` unconditionally allocate new `{ ...type }` objects even when every child maps to itself (`===`).
  2. In `substitute` (`substitution.ts:30`), `if (type.alias?.kind === 'associated')` calls `rules.associatedType(substitute(type.alias.of, ...), type.alias.name)` even when `type.alias.of` is concrete and unchanged, and `associatedTypeOf` (`known-constraints.ts:195`) is unmemoized — re-running `aliasInstance` and allocating thousands of duplicate `Field<String>.Seen`, `Group<CategoryModel>.Seen`, and `String?` objects.
  3. Every newly allocated `Type` object misses the `WeakMap<Type, ...>` caches `ownKeys` (`type-keys.ts`) and `recursionHeld` (`type-children.ts`), and when `rebuiltBy` calls `unionOf` on a union holding recursion, `folded` (`recursive-types.ts:279`) builds and minimizes a full `TermGraph` from scratch.
  4. `parametersIn(type)` (`type-parts.ts:44`) is unmemoized, and `instantiatedBody` (`instantiation.ts:39`) does not memoize `substitute` per `Type` or reuse instantiated bodies across nested lambdas in `frames.ts`.
- **Proposed Root Fix (medium):** Return `type` unchanged (`===`) in `mapParts`, `rebuiltBy`, `asWritten`, and `substitute`'s `associated` alias branch when children/owner are `===`; memoize `parametersIn` and `unaliased` in `WeakMap`s; memoize top-level `associatedType` in `program-type-rules.ts` when `associating.length === 0 && reductions.isIdle`; and memoize `concrete` in `instantiatedBody`.

### 3. [NEW] `TypeParameters.checkNoted(module)` Re-Runs All Module-Wide Deferred Constraint Checks Outside `Entries` on Every Keystroke
- **Phase & Workload:** Language Service / Incremental Checker (`Workspace.write` + `diagnosticsOf` on `examples/FormBuilder` `supplier.gen.ista`).
- **Cost:** **91.39% of incremental edit time** on `supplier.gen.ista` (`994 ms / 1,078 ms` single edit; ~749 ms / 835 ms median over 6 edits).
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/compiler/checker/resolution/type-parameters.ts`: `checkNoted`, `#check`, `#noted`
  - `src/compiler/checker/declarations/module-checks.ts`: `ModuleChecks` (`#modules` table)
- **Mechanism:** When any single function body in `module` is edited, the module's `'module checks'` entry recomputes. Although all 55 unchanged items' `'item checks'` entries are taken over from `previous`, `checkNoted(module)` collects every `{ kind: 'constraint', module, check }` note from `entries.notesReached()` across the entire module and runs `this.#check(module, check)` directly inside `'module checks'` instead of through an `EntryTable`. Every deferred constraint check in the module is re-executed from scratch on every edit.
- **Proposed Root Fix (medium):** Wrap `this.#check(module, check)` in an `EntryTable` in `TypeParameters` (or run item-local constraint checks inside `item checks` entries) so unchanged items' constraint checks are taken over across revisions.

### 4. [NEW] `written-matches.ts` & `type-parameters.ts` Read `checker.inputs.treeOf(module)` During Checking, Invalidating 133–400 `#resolvedTypes` Entries and Rebuilding Whole-Tree `parentsIn(tree)` on Every Edit
- **Phase & Workload:** Incremental checking (`edit.bench.ts` 2,098-line module, 16x 4,710-line scaled module, and `examples/FormBuilder`).
- **Cost:** **14.59% of total incremental edit time** at 16x (4,710 lines) and **10.37%** on `edit.bench.ts` (2,098 lines) in `matchPlaceOf` -> `parentOf` -> `parentsIn`; forces **133 of 538 `#resolvedTypes` entries** (2,098 lines) and **400 of 1,737 `#resolvedTypes` entries** (`FormBuilder`) to fail `#replays` and recompute on a 1-character body edit.
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/compiler/checker/types/written/written-matches.ts`: `inCasePattern`, `asWrittenInMatch`, `insideMatch`, `treeOf`
  - `src/compiler/syntax/types/type-matches.ts`: `matchPlaceOf`
  - `src/compiler/syntax/tree.ts`: `parentOf`, `parentsIn`, `nodesUnder`
  - `src/compiler/checker/resolution/type-parameters.ts`: `impliedConstraints` calls in `#constraints` and `#structConstraints`
- **Mechanism:** `argumentsOf` (`resolve-type.ts:332`) calls `inCasePattern(resolution, node)` on every generic `NamedType`, and `namedType` calls `asWrittenInMatch` on every `TypeParameter`. Both read `checker.inputs.treeOf(module)` (whose `equal` is `Object.is(oldTree, newTree)`, always `false` after any edit!), violating `ProgramInputs`'s contract (`"checking reads itemsOf"`). Furthermore, `matchPlaceOf(tree, node)` calls `parentOf(tree, node)` -> `parentsIn(tree)`, which walks every node of every function body in the module (`nodesUnder(tree, tree.root)`) on every edit, even though `TypeMatch` only ever exists in `TypeAliasDeclaration` or `AssociatedType` headers (`itemsOf`).
- **Proposed Root Fix (medium):** Derive parent maps per immutable item/body node via `fieldChildrenOf` (memoized in a `WeakMap<SyntaxNode, ReadonlyMap<SyntaxNode, SyntaxNode>>`) and read `inputs.itemsOf(module)` (or pass `matchPlace` down `Resolution`) instead of `inputs.treeOf(module)` during checking.

### 5. [Confirming P1 / P3 / P4 + NEW `test:std` Finding] Whole-Snapshot Zod + Lossless-JSON Round-Trip (`write` + `read`) on Every Commit and Repeated `Definition` Parsing in `runTests`
- **Phase & Workload:** Runtime / Scheduler (`wake.bench.ts` and `npm run test:std`).
- **Cost:** **76.56% of `wake.bench.ts`** (514 ms at 100 workers -> 1,862 ms at 200 workers, scaling exponent **$b = 1.86$**), plus **9.25%** in `lifetimes/holders.ts:reached` (P3/P4). On `npm run test:std` (1,510 ms total), **38.6% of total time** (`573 ms`) is inside Zod (`inst._zod.run`, `inst._zod.parse`, `memoizer.wrapped`) and **16.8% self** in `definition/codec.ts`.
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/runtime/scheduler/wake.ts`: `commit`, `runFrom`
  - `src/definition/format.ts`: `write`, `read`
  - `src/definition/codec.ts`: `written`, `textDepth`, `jsonDepth`
  - `src/runtime/testing/world/index.ts`: `TestWorld` (`runtime.deploy(definition)`)
- **Mechanism:** Every `commit()` serializes the entire heap snapshot to canonical JSON through Zod (`snapshotFormat.write`) and immediately parses and re-validates it from text (`snapshotFormat.read`) for the next segment (P1). Additionally (**NEW**), in `runtime/testing/world/index.ts`, every single `@Test` creates a new `TestWorld` that calls `runtime.deploy(definitionText)`, re-parsing and Zod-validating the entire compiled `Definition` JSON string once per test across all std tests.
- **Proposed Root Fix (medium/big):** Restore the next segment from the in-memory `Snapshot` in `commit()` (P1), and parse/validate `definitionText` once in `runTests` rather than once per `TestWorld`.

### 6. [NEW] Formatter `trailingDocs` Unconditionally Walks the Entire Prettier `Doc` Subtree via `suffixWaitsAfter` at Every AST Node Even When `trailing` Is Empty
- **Phase & Workload:** Formatter (`formatSource` / `formatTree` on `examples/FormBuilder` + `src/stdlib`).
- **Cost:** **8.27–8.66% self time / 16.45–18.19% total time** of the entire formatter (nearly equal to Prettier's own `printDocToString` at 18.3%).
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/formatter/comments.ts`: `trailingDocs` (line 89), `suffixWaitsAfter` (line 96), `holdsLineSuffix` (line 125)
  - `src/formatter/format.ts`: `TreePrinter.withComments` (line 86)
- **Mechanism:** `TreePrinter.withComments(node, doc)` calls `trailingDocs(trailing, doc)` on every AST node. `trailingDocs` runs `let lineTaken = suffixWaitsAfter(doc, false);` before checking if `comments` (`trailing`) is empty. Because `suffixWaitsAfter` recursively walks the entire Prettier `Doc` subtree of `node`, every `Doc` node at depth $d$ is walked $d$ times ($O(N \cdot D)$) even when `trailing.length === 0` (>99% of AST nodes).
- **Proposed Root Fix (easy):** In `trailingDocs` (`src/formatter/comments.ts:93`), return `[]` immediately when `comments.length === 0` (or compute `suffixWaitsAfter` lazily only when `comments` has a non-`ownLine` comment).

### 7. [Confirming P21 + NEW `fieldChildrenOf` Details] Generic `Object.values(node)` Reflection in `bodiesHeldBy` and `fieldChildrenOf`
- **Phase & Workload:** Parser (`scan-parse.cpuprofile`), Formatter (`formatter.cpuprofile`), and Incremental Edit.
- **Cost:** `bodiesHeldBy` is **12.17% self / 13.49% total** of `scan-parse.cpuprofile` (P21); `fieldChildrenOf` + `nodesUnder` + `parentsIn` is **11.59% self / 22.58% total** of 16x incremental edit and **6.55% self / 14.01% total (`copiedSpans`)** of `formatter.cpuprofile`.
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/compiler/parser/held-bodies.ts`: `bodiesHeldBy`
  - `src/compiler/syntax/tree.ts`: `fieldChildrenOf`, `nodesUnder`, `parentsIn`
- **Mechanism:** `bodiesHeldBy` walks every AST node (including leaves, types, and patterns) via `Object.values(node)` and `.filter(isNode)` even when `parser.diagnostics.length === 0` (where no recovery occurred and `parser.recordedBodies` already holds the exact map). `fieldChildrenOf` allocates 4 intermediate arrays (`Object.values`, `flatMap`, two `filter`s, `toSorted`, spread) per AST node.
- **Proposed Root Fix (easy/medium):** Fast-path `parser.diagnostics.length === 0` in `parse.ts` to use `parser.recordedBodies` directly; restrict `bodiesHeldBy` to node kinds that can hold functions; and streamline `fieldChildrenOf` to a single loop.

### 8. [NEW] Language Service `referencesAt` / `renameEdits` Force-Checks All `isStd` Module Bodies and Walks Ancestors 3x per Identifier
- **Phase & Workload:** Language Service Navigation (`referencesAt` and `renameEdits` on `examples/FormBuilder` and 1x/4x/16x scaling).
- **Cost:** **~666 ms** on `FormBuilder` (`87.4% total` in `lookup.ts:#itemChecked`) and **~179 ms** even on a 300-line module (`1x` scale).
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/language-service/navigation/references.ts`: `indexOf`, `namesDenoting`, `referencesAt`
  - `src/language-service/navigation/declarations.ts`: `actedOnBy`, `declarationsOf`
  - `src/compiler/checker/lookup/lookup.ts`: `denotationsOf`, `#itemChecked`
  - `src/compiler/checker/lookup/holders.ts`: `itemHolding`, `bodyHolding`, `ancestorsOf`
- **Mechanism:** `references.ts:isName` skips `StringLiteral`s in `isStd` modules ("reading its literals would check each of its bodies"), but does NOT skip `Identifier`s in `isStd` modules! Thus `indexOf(program)` calls `actedOnBy` -> `lookup.denotationsOf` on every identifier in every `isStd` module, which calls `#itemChecked` and `bodyHolding` — force-checking every stdlib function body even when finding references to a user declaration (which std modules can never reference). Additionally, `lookup.denotationsOf` calls `ancestorsOf(tree, name)` 3 times per identifier and `itemHolding` builds `new Set(ancestorsOf(tree, node))` on every call.
- **Proposed Root Fix (medium):** Skip `isStd` modules in `references.ts` when the target declaration is not in `isStd`; compute `ancestorsOf` once in `lookup.denotationsOf` and find `itemHolding` as `ancestors.at(-2)` in $O(1)$.

### 9. [Confirming P10 / P14 / P19 + NEW `allTokensOf` & `notesOfReads` Details] 3x Full-Module Scan, 2x Parse & 2x Bind in `completionsAt`, Plus Uncached `notesOfReads` DAG Walk
- **Phase & Workload:** Language Service Completion (`ls-completion-after-edit.cpuprofile`) and Incremental Edit (`ls-edit-2098.cpuprofile`).
- **Cost:** `completionsAt` after an edit takes **44.5 ms median** on 2,098 lines and **129.5 ms** at 16x (4,710 lines, scaling exponent $b = 0.96$). In `ls-completion-after-edit.cpuprofile`, **38.25%** is `parser` + `scanner`, **12.09% self** is `binder` (P10), and in `ls-edit-2098.cpuprofile`, `notesOfReads` (`structures/entries/entry.ts:26`) is **4.52% self** (#2 non-GC function).
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/language-service/completion/places/grammar.ts`: `offerGrammar` -> `src/language-service/completion/placeholder.ts`: `parsedWithPlaceholder` (P14)
  - `src/language-service/tokens.ts`: `allTokensOf`, `flatten`
  - `src/structures/entries/entry.ts`: `notesOfReads`, `notesFrom`
  - `src/structures/entries/index.ts`: `#replays`
- **Mechanism:** Completing after an edit scans the file 3 times and parses/binds it twice: (1) `Workspace` scans + parses + binds the edited text; (2) `cursorAt` -> `allTokensOf(tree)` (`language-service/tokens.ts`) re-scans `tree.source` and runs `pairBrackets` again because `SyntaxTree` drops `tokens`; (3) `parsedWithPlaceholder` (`placeholder.ts`) inserts `__ista_completion__` and re-scans, re-parses, and re-binds the whole module (P14). Meanwhile, `notesOfReads` (`entry.ts:26`) walks the transitive DAG of `entry.reads` from scratch on every `notesFrom` / `#readNotes` without memoizing on completed entries.
- **Proposed Root Fix (small/medium):** Memoize `notesFrom` on completed entries (`!entry.running`); keep `tokens` on `SyntaxTree` (or memoize `scan(source)`); and parse only the cursor's segment in `placeholder.ts` (P14).

### 10. [NEW] Language Service `inlayHintsIn` Calls `extentIn(tree, node)` (`SequenceMemo`) on Every AST Node in a Recursive `flatMap` Walk
- **Phase & Workload:** Language Service Inlay Hints (`ls-inlay-formbuilder.cpuprofile` and 1x/4x/16x scaling).
- **Cost:** **24.33% self / 38.11% total** of `ls-inlay-formbuilder.cpuprofile` in `untypedNames` (`src/language-service/inlay-hints.ts:30`), plus **9.97% self** in `memo` (`SequenceMemo.get`), taking **20.6–24.4 ms** at 16x (4,710 lines).
- **Hot Functions:**
  - `src/language-service/inlay-hints.ts`: `untypedNames` (line 30), `declaredType` (line 44)
  - `src/compiler/syntax/tree.ts`: `extentIn` (`SequenceMemo.get`)
- **Mechanism:** `untypedNames(tree, node, span)` calls `extentIn(tree, node)` (which performs a 2-step `WeakMap` lookup in `SequenceMemo` and walks to first/last leaf descendants when uncached) and allocates intermediate `flatMap` arrays at every single AST node, even after `span` already encloses the containing top-level item.
- **Proposed Root Fix (easy):** Once `encloses(span, extentIn(tree, node))` holds (or when `span` covers the module), walk descendants with a flat stack without calling `extentIn` on every child node.

## Ruled Out & Measurement Caveats

### Ruled Out
1. **Formatter `format.bench.ts` exponential hugging (depth 16):** Measured at **0.53 ms / iter** — `TreePrinter.print`'s per-node `#docs` memoization completely prevents exponential blowup on nested hugged calls.
2. **Runtime `host.bench.ts` (`inspect` a finished instance):** `Definition` decoding is cached per `Definition` text, so `inspect` only decodes the instance's own snapshot.
3. **Member completion (`P13`) on normal-sized receivers:** On `d3(x).` in the 2,098-line module (`ls-completion-member.cpuprofile`), member completion takes **~0.85 ms / iter** (17.3 ms over 20 iterations), well within the 15 ms budget; `offerGrammar` placeholder re-parsing (P14) is the actual completion bottleneck.
4. **Scanner & Binder scaling:** Both scale linearly ($b \approx 0.46\text{–}0.97$) and together account for **< 2.5%** of full compile time on `examples/FormBuilder` (`scanner`: 0.40%, `binder`: 1.46%).

### Measurement Caveats
1. **Concurrent machine load:** Other agents run test suites on this machine concurrently, causing wall-clock variance (~10–20% spread across runs). All rankings above are based on **V8 CPU profile share (`selfPct` / `totalPct` of active CPU samples)**, **exact call/allocation counts**, and **scaling exponents ($1\times, 4\times, 16\times$)** rather than single wall-clock timings.
2. **Inspector boundary samples:** `node:inspector` `Profiler.stop` can record a single sample on `node:inspector:post` spanning idle boundary time; `scripts/profile/cpu-profile.ts` filters `node:inspector`, `(idle)`, and `(program)` frames from `activeUs`.
3. **Reusable Profiler Script:** Saved at `scripts/profile/toolchain.ts` (with `scripts/profile/cpu-profile.ts`); run with `npm run profile -- [output-dir]`.
